    A real wired line, not a radio signal

    Nexi Net Fiber vs Airtel Xstream AirFiber

    AirFiber is wireless broadband beamed to a router — useful where fiber can't reach yet, but it's built on different physics than a dedicated fiber line to your door.

    Nexi Net · Wired Fiber

    OUR LINE
    • ✓Dedicated line to your home — speed isn't shared with neighbours on a tower.
    • ✓Unaffected by weather or walls — rain, buildings and distance don't touch a fibre line.
    • ✓Stable low latency — built for gaming, video calls and live streaming.
    • ✓Consistent speed at peak hours — no tower congestion when everyone's online at once.
    • ✓Scales cleanly to higher plans — 50 Mbps up to 200 Mbps on the same fibre.

    Airtel Xstream AirFiber

    WIRELESS
    • ✕Signal is shared with other users on the same tower/cell — speed can dip in busy hours.
    • ✕Performance depends on tower distance, walls and router placement — indoor units need a clear window/line of sight.
    • ✕Weather (heavy rain, storms) and network congestion can affect wireless signal quality.
    • ✕Upload speeds and real-world speed are usually well below the advertised number, unlike a symmetric wired line.
    • ✕Meant as a fill-in where wired fibre hasn't reached yet — not a fibre replacement.

    General characteristics of fixed-wireless (AirFiber-type) technology vs wired fibre — actual AirFiber performance can vary by plan and location.
